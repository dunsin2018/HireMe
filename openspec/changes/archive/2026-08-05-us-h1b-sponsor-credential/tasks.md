Numbers recorded on 2026-08-04, via a throwaway measurement program (not committed, deleted after use):
  - **First live run failed outright**: `FetchUSH1BSponsors` errored on the real FY2019 file with `csv has no "Initial Approval" column` — USCIS pluralizes that column (and its three siblings) in older fiscal-year files, a schema drift the FY2023-only unit fixtures never exercised. Fixed in a follow-up commit (`ParseUSH1BSponsorsCSV` now falls back to the plural spelling), with a new test using a real excerpt of the live FY2019 file. This is exactly the class of bug this measurement step exists to catch before a write.
  - **After the fix**: fetched 255,198 raw records across the 5 most recent fiscal years (FY2019–FY2023 at measurement time); 217,599 rows survive `DropAmbiguous`.
  - **Catalogue reachability constraint**: the public `/companies` endpoint reports **58,076** total US-hiring companies, but `offset + limit` may not exceed 10,000 (`pagination too deep`) — so only the top 10,000 (ranked "most active first") were reachable for this audit, not a full census like the UK (7,944, fully covered) and NL (2,732, fully covered) runs. This is a real, structural gap in this audit's coverage, not a shortcut taken casually — noted here rather than silently treated as equivalent to the UK/NL census.
  - **Outcome on the reachable 10,000**: **matched 1,098**, gated 1,126. Top 40 alphabetical grants are plausible real US legal entities (Airbnb, Akamai Technologies, Affirm, ABM Industries, Ace Hardware, AES Corporation, 10x Genomics, among others) — no false positive found in that sample.
  - Also discovered: `docs/API.md`'s documented `/companies` list response shape (with `countries`, `regions`, `domains`, etc.) does not match what the live endpoint actually returns today (a six-field projection: slug, name, job_count, tagline, industries, hq_country, collections — no `countries` field) — pre-existing drift, unrelated to this change, not fixed here as it's out of scope.
